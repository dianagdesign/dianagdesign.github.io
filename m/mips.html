A Medicare quality platform clinicians would recommend.

I moved design ahead of the sprint

Each year, clinicians report quality measures to Medicare, and part of their payment depends on the score. It is a compliance obligation that shows up as a daily workflow problem. I designed it so teams always knew what to do next and could submit with confidence. Designing a sprint ahead helped cut development cycles from four weeks to two, and we hit the Medicare deadline.

I designed the quality dashboards with input from the boards of two specialty societies.

Make the next step obvious

Clear status on every measure, so teams can see what is done, what is at risk and what needs attention now.

Show everything, but structure it so the important signals rise to the top.

Put the rule next to the decision

People used to leave the page to look up the data completeness rule, so I put it right where the number is entered.

9/10likelihood to recommend, asked of users after launch
~3,000clinicians reported to Medicare through it

Nearly 3,000 clinicians across two specialty registries submitted their Medicare data through the platform, the first year practices could choose from every available measure.

What I’d carry forward. Compliance tools don’t need less information. They need a clear order, so the next step is always obvious.
